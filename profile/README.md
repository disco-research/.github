DISCO is a research group in the [School of Interactive Games and Media](https://www.rit.edu/computing/school-interactive-games-and-media) of the [Golisano College of Computing and Information Sciences](https://www.rit.edu/computing/) at [RIT](https://www.rit.edu/). We build mobile and immersive computing systems that adapt to the people who use them, the devices they run on, the environments around them, and the AI models inside them.

## Research

Computing is moving off the screen and into physical spaces. For that to work, systems have to understand the real world accurately, keep up with changing environments, and respond to what users are doing. Our work covers:

- **Mobile augmented reality**: lighting estimation, depth estimation, and rendering for AR on mobile devices.
- **AR experimentation and evaluation**: tools to capture, replay, and compare AR sensor data and experiments.
- **Dynamic intelligence**: AI systems that adapt to real-world conditions on resource-limited devices.

See our [publications](https://disco.rit.edu/publication/) for the full list.
